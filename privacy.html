Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 26, 2026

Ziyada is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the Ziyada mobile application.

01 Information We Collect

Ziyada does not require an account, a sign-up, or an email address.

Stored on your device only

  • Your optional display name
  • Quiz progress, scores, streak, and series progress
  • Appearance and reminder preferences
  • A random device identifier, created on first launch

Usage and diagnostic data

To understand which parts of the app are working, we collect the following through our analytics provider (see Section 04):

  • Screens visited, session frequency and length, and in-app taps (for anonymous heatmaps)
  • Device model, OS, app version, and country-level location derived from IP Address
  • Crash reports and performance diagnostics
  • Six app events — onboarding, quiz started/abandoned/completed, streak recorded, reminder toggled — with only identifiers, scores, and true/false values

This is tied to your device identifier, never your name. We don't use it to advertise to you or combine it with other apps' data.

What we never collect

Your display name (device-only), quiz question or answer text, contacts, photos, precise location, health data, or payment info. Screen recording is off.

02 How We Use Information

To improve performance, understand feature usage, and fix bugs — nothing more. We don't sell, rent, or share it beyond our analytics provider (Section 04), and we never build an advertising profile of you.

03 Offline-First Experience

Every quiz and lesson ships inside the app, so no internet is needed for any feature. The app only goes online to send the usage data described in Section 01 — offline, it works exactly the same.

04 Analytics and Third-Party Services

We use one third party: Vexo, a mobile analytics provider, to process the usage data from Section 01 on our behalf — see the Vexo privacy policy. There are no ad networks, social SDKs, or data brokers in Ziyada.

05 Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Since most data lives on your device, your device's own security (screen lock, encryption) matters too.

06 Your Choices and Deletion

  • Settings → "Clear My Data" permanently erases your quiz history, streak, and progress
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored locally, including your display name and device identifier
  • No account is ever required
  • Email us (Section 09) to request deletion of data held by our analytics provider

If you're in the EEA, UK, or a region with similar laws, email us to request access to or deletion of your data.

07 Children's Privacy

Ziyada does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is designed for general audiences.

08 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated "Effective Date."
